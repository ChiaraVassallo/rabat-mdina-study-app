# Rabat & Mdina Study App

Open `Open Study App.bat` to launch the study app in your browser.

This prototype now includes:

- A polished study dashboard inspired by the reference layout
- Location cards for the five tour sites
- Numbered internal stops imported from the Rabat cheat sheet
- A focused location page with hero image, tabs, route map, stop list, and selected stop panel
- Placeholder sections ready for your future notes, photos, videos, scripts, and exam prompts

Next, replace the starter notes in `app.js` with your real course notes, photos, or tour material.
